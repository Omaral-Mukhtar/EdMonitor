# AI Educathon 2026 — One-Page Solution Outline

Team: Cherry



| Section | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| Problem | Freshman Computer Science undergraduates frequently lose concentration to phone pickups and desk drift during solo study, which is especially challenging for students with attention difficulties or ADHD and lowers overall study quality. Students and Teaching Assistants also struggle to coordinate discussion sessions across shared groups and rooms without creating timetable conflicts. Meanwhile, generic AI chatbots often give unverified answers or use methods outside introductory university math courses. |
| Learner / persona | The primary learners are freshman Computer Science undergraduates at Alamein International University taking foundational math and computing courses, including students who struggle with focus or ADHD and need structured, distraction-aware study routines. Secondary users are Teaching Assistants who manage and lead weekly discussion hours. These learners study on standard laptops over shared campus or home internet and face focus fatigue during assignment and exam periods. |
| Solution | Team Cherry will build EdMonitor, a web-based study platform with three connected parts. First, a Teaching Assistant discussion scheduler will prevent timetable conflicts across student groups, instructors, and rooms while suggesting open slots. Second, a virtual campus map will connect quiet solo rooms and topic-based study rooms featuring study timers, optional peer voice and text study tables, and an optional camera-based focus assistant that improves study quality and supports students with ADHD through gentle, automatic focus pauses. Third, a private math whiteboard will offer a syllabus-guided AI tutor and accurate graph plotting. |
| AI contribution | On-device computer vision will recognize phone pickups, desk absence, and prolonged loss of attention to gently pause the study timer and help students, especially those with ADHD or focus fatigue, build stronger study habits without storing any video. Separately, a math-focused AI tutor will review a student's hand-drawn whiteboard sketch, point out setup mistakes within the course syllabus, and guide the student step by step. Instead of rough or hallucinated drawings, the tutor will generate accurate step-by-step graphs aligned with exact course equations. |
| Success metric | Learning outcomes will be measured through higher focus percentages and fewer distractions per study block, alongside more consistent daily study habits and completed study minutes. Platform reliability will be measured through zero scheduling conflicts for Teaching Assistant sessions and accurate, syllabus-aligned math explanations and graphs. |
| Risks and ethics | Data privacy: focus detection will run strictly on the student's own device with zero video saved or uploaded, camera use will remain completely optional, and demonstrations will use fictional accounts. Bias and fairness: course-syllabus rules will keep math guidance consistent and accurate, while focus alerts will remain supportive and non-punitive with a short grace period and break exemptions designed to support rather than diagnose or penalize students with ADHD. Accessibility: students will have full manual timer mode when the camera is off, high-contrast whiteboard controls, quiet study rooms without mandatory voice or video, and simple web access. Digital sobriety: lightweight browser-based 3D rooms with no heavy downloads, automatic pausing of 3D visuals when not in view, and compact AI responses will keep energy and computing use low. |
| Next steps | During the hackathon on Saturday 28 November 2026, Team Cherry will build the core student login and conflict-free Teaching Assistant discussion scheduler alongside the virtual campus map with quiet and collaborative study rooms and focus timers. We will integrate on-device camera focus detection and add the private math AI whiteboard with accurate graphing. Finally, we will run an end-to-end prototype demonstration using fictional student data to validate the full workflow. |
