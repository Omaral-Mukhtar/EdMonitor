# Learner Persona

## Profile
- **Name:** Youssef (20)
- **Occupation:** University Undergraduate (Higher Education)
- **Quote:** *"I put in the hours and pick a study method, yet distractions break my flow and abstract concepts still don't click."*

---

## Personality
- Ambitious
- Self-Aware
- Visual Thinker
- Easily Distracted
- Time-Conscious
- Tech-Savvy

## Goals & Needs
- **Improve learning quality** by turning abstract concepts across any subject into clear, structured visuals that build real understanding.
- **Maximize time efficiency** by turning long desk hours into genuinely focused, high-yield study blocks.
- **Execute study styles properly** (Pomodoro, Deep Work, Feynman, Flowtime) with live guardrails and peer accountability.

## Frustrations
- **Method-execution gap:** Chooses a proven study technique but drifts off-cycle and fails to apply it correctly.
- **Poor distraction control:** Reflexive phone checks and look-aways silently break focus while standard timers keep counting.
- **Weak visual clarity:** Dense text and rough sketches leave complex university topics blurry, forcing slow rote memorization.

## Behavior
- **Studies on a standard laptop** from a dorm, home desk, or library—mostly solo, where isolation accelerates focus drop-off.
- **Experiments with study methods and timers**, but abandons them mid-session when fatigue or phone notifications hit.
- **Sketches rough diagrams or searches for visuals** when stuck, losing valuable time trying to make abstract material click.

---

> **Success Criteria:** Higher **learning quality** (clearer visual mastery across general subjects) and better **time efficiency** (more genuinely focused minutes per session).
